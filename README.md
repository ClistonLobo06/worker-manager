# ⚡ Lobo Electricals - Worker & Salary Manager

An offline-first Progressive Web App (PWA) designed to manage electrical worker daily attendance, wage calculations, weekly payment settlements, site notes, and quick arithmetic calculations on desktop and mobile devices.

## 🚀 Features

- **Daily Attendance Sheet**: Track presence, work site location, and custom daily wages per worker.
- **Weekly Salary Settlement**: Automatically compute weekly bills, track amounts paid, calculate remaining balances, and generate printable payment slips.
- **Worker Management**: Add, view, and organize workers by work category or site.
- **Site & Business Notes**: Maintain site notes, material purchase lists, and customer payment records.
- **Quick Calculator**: Integrated floating calculator for quick on-site arithmetic.
- **100% Offline Support (PWA)**: Works completely offline using Service Workers and browser local storage.
- **Backup & Restore**: Easily export and import database snapshots as `.json` files.

## 📱 Mobile Installation

1. Open the live site URL in your mobile browser (**Google Chrome** on Android or **Safari** on iOS).
2. Tap **"Install App"** (Chrome) or **"Add to Home Screen"** (Safari Share menu).
3. The app is added directly to your home screen and operates seamlessly offline.
