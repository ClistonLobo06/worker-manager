📲 Install to Phone Home Screen

Google Chrome on Android:

  • 1 Make sure you are in Google Chrome browser.
  • 2 Tap the three dots (⋮) in the top-right corner of Chrome.
  • 3 Look for and tap "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
  • 4 Tap Install. The ⚡ Lobo Electricals icon will appear on your phone's home screen!

Samsung Internet users: Tap ☰ (Menu) at bottom-right > "Add page to" > "Home screen".

Apple Safari on iPhone:

  • 1 Open this link in Safari browser.
  • 2 Tap the Share button (square with arrow pointing up ⎋) at the bottom toolbar.
  • 3 Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen" (➕).
  • 4 Tap Add in the top-right corner. Done!

⚠️ If you tapped the link inside WhatsApp:

  • 1 WhatsApp opens links in a temporary browser that blocks app installations.
  • 2 Look at the very top-right corner of this screen for 3 dots (⋮).
  • 3 Tap the 3 dots, then tap "Open in Chrome" (or "Open in Safari" / "Open in browser").
  • 4 Once in Chrome, tap "📲 Install App"!
